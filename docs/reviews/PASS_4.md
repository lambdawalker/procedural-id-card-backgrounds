## Executive Summary
Pass 4 audited the distinctiveness and execution of regional cultural, botanical, and architectural motifs across the ten states. The goal was to confirm that each state possesses an unmistakable visual signature completely distinct from simple palette variations.

---

## Per-State Visual Inspection Findings

### 1. Alaska (AK)
- **Motif Inspection**: Aurora Borealis wave curtains and braided glacial stream patterns create an unmistakable arctic atmosphere.
- **Findings**: Distinct from all other mountain designs. Satisfactory.

### 2. Arkansas (AR)
- **Motif Inspection**: The 8-point diamond rosette guilloche reflects the state's famous Crater of Diamonds heritage. The rolling Ozark hills provide regional topography.
- **Findings**: Geometry is clean and motif is uniquely identifying for Arkansas.

### 3. Colorado (CO)
- **Motif Inspection**: 48-ray radial sunrise guilloche fan rising from behind multi-tier Rocky Mountains provides an authentic Colorado DMV aesthetic.
- **Findings**: Contrast balance between sunbeams and snowcapped peaks is optimal.

### 4. Idaho (ID)
- **Motif Inspection**: Sawtooth mountain serrations combined with concentric agricultural furrow arcs and evergreen conifer silhouettes.
- **Findings**: The intersection of alpine wilderness and agricultural basin is well-represented.

### 5. Illinois (IL)
- **Motif Inspection**: Classical fluted pillars with Ionic capital volutes and entablatures bring civic neoclassical architecture to life on the card's left margin.
- **Findings**: Columns provide architectural structure that sets Illinois apart from natural landscape cards.

### 6. Louisiana (LA)
- **Motif Inspection**: Wrought-iron balcony scrollwork in the header with repeating S-curves and central rosettes evokes the French Quarter. Meandering delta waterways complement the architecture.
- **Findings**: Strongest architectural identity in the southern region.

### 7. Tennessee (TN)
- **Motif Inspection**: Great Smoky Mountains misty ridges paired with a 5-line musical notation staff wave and botanical dogwood flowers.
- **Findings**: The musical staff is cleanly drawn with zero clashing against background contours.

### 8. Utah (UT)
- **Motif Inspection**: Delicate Arch sandstone bridge structure with open negative space window cutout, backed by red rock strata and Wasatch peaks.
- **Findings**: Geological arch silhouette is sharp, accurate, and regionally unmistakable.

### 9. Washington (WA)
- **Motif Inspection**: Volcanic peak profile of Mount Rainier, coastal evergreen conifer tree stands, and marine wave guilloche representing Puget Sound.
- **Findings**: Balanced maritime/alpine composition.

### 10. Wyoming (WY)
- **Motif Inspection**: Sharp, jagged Grand Teton peaks, High Plains wind streamline vectors, and western engraved scrollwork corner flourishes.
- **Findings**: Western security document motif is distinct and authentic.
