## Executive Summary
Pass 6 inspected line sharpness, micro-engraving strokes, and banknote-style security hatchwork across all ten target state backgrounds. Micro-linework and fine stroke widths (0.5px to 0.8px) were evaluated at full resolution to verify that vector rendering produces clean, un-aliased engraving without blur or heavy black buildup.

---

## Per-State Line Quality Findings

### 1. Alaska (AK)
- **Line Quality**: Glacial meltwater ribbons and aurora sine waves maintain 0.65px - 0.75px stroke widths.
- **Findings**: Clean, crisp edge definitions. Denali ridge highlight line (1.2px) provides strong alpine definition.

### 2. Arkansas (AR)
- **Line Quality**: Diamond rosette facets and concentric diamond rings display banknote-grade precision.
- **Findings**: The 8 radiating facet lines intersect precisely at the rosette apex with zero misalignment.

### 3. Colorado (CO)
- **Line Quality**: 48 radial sunrise fan rays expand outward with consistent 0.6px line weights.
- **Findings**: Ray endpoints end cleanly at outer perimeter without bleeding into card borders.

### 4. Idaho (ID)
- **Line Quality**: Conifer branches and agricultural furrow lines exhibit razor-sharp vector delineation.
- **Findings**: Furrow curves remain smooth and continuous across the horizontal span.

### 5. Illinois (IL)
- **Line Quality**: Column shaft fluting lines (0.75px) mimic classical stone masonry flutes.
- **Findings**: Capital volute spiral curves are smooth Bezier segments without polygonal angularity.

### 6. Louisiana (LA)
- **Line Quality**: Wrought-iron S-scrolls and C-scrolls maintain consistent 0.75px line weight.
- **Findings**: Balcony rail lines and panel dividing rods align horizontally and vertically.

### 7. Tennessee (TN)
- **Line Quality**: 5-line musical staff waves render with 0.8px stroke width in warm gold.
- **Findings**: Sinusoidal curves maintain uniform vertical 6px staff spacing along their entire trajectory.

### 8. Utah (UT)
- **Line Quality**: Sandstone strata lines (0.75px) cross the center with subtle harmonic erosion waviness.
- **Findings**: Delicate Arch boundary outline (1.0px) separates the arch monolith cleanly from distant strata.

### 9. Washington (WA)
- **Line Quality**: Puget Sound maritime wave clusters show distinct phase-shifted sinusoidal lines.
- **Findings**: Fir tree tiered silhouettes provide clean vertical contrast against horizontal wave ribbons.

### 10. Wyoming (WY)
- **Line Quality**: Wind streamlines (0.5px) utilize alternating dash intervals (`14 6 4 6`) to emulate vector airflow.
- **Findings**: Western engraved corner flourishes add authentic security document craftsmanship.
