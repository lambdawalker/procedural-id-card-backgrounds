## Executive Summary
In this initial review pass, all ten target state procedural designs were generated, rendered to SVG, converted to high-resolution PNGs at 1000x630, and compiled into a unified 10-card contact sheet. The goal of Pass 1 was to establish distinct, reference-inspired compositions across all states, verify core layouts, and ensure no template homogeny.

---

## Per-State Visual Inspection Findings

### 1. Alaska (AK)
- **Composition & Layout**: Denali massif rising across the center-left horizon, framed by an undulating Aurora Borealis wave curtain in the upper sky and braided glacial river curves in the lower third.
- **Findings**: The Denali massif silhouette is well-proportioned. The glacial river ribbons in the lower third are slightly sparse; increasing line count will better simulate glacial braided stream beds.
- **Next Steps**: Enhance glacial braided river density and refine aurora ribbon frequency modulation.

### 2. Arkansas (AR)
- **Composition & Layout**: Central geometric 8-point diamond rosette celebrating Crater of Diamonds State Park, backed by rolling Ozark hill contours and meandering river curves.
- **Findings**: The diamond rosette is crisp and well-centered. The Ozark hill contours give good background depth.
- **Next Steps**: Smooth out river curve transitions and add subtle secondary guilloche framing.

### 3. Colorado (CO)
- **Composition & Layout**: Multi-tier Rocky Mountain range featuring a distant atmospheric ridge and sharp foreground peak, backed by a 48-ray radial sunrise fan and valley floor grid.
- **Findings**: Mountain ridges and sunrise rays create an authentic alpine security document feel. The sunbeam ray opacity needs slight attenuation to preserve high contrast for text overlay fields.
- **Next Steps**: Balance sunrise ray opacity and calibrate foreground perspective grid.

### 4. Idaho (ID)
- **Composition & Layout**: Sharp, serrated Sawtooth mountain ridgeline across the upper horizon, tiered foothill conifers, and rhythmic agricultural field furrows across the lower half.
- **Findings**: The Sawtooth serrations provide a distinct, rugged profile. The conifer silhouettes along the foothill base anchor the landscape effectively.
- **Next Steps**: Refine agricultural furrow expansion and fine-tune pine tree spacing.

### 5. Illinois (IL)
- **Composition & Layout**: Classical architectural fluted columns and ionic capitals along the left margin, rolling prairie horizon lines across the midground, and Lake Michigan water waves in the upper right.
- **Findings**: The fluted column geometry provides a distinct framing structure that sets Illinois apart from mountainous states. Prairie lines in the lower right are clean and subtle.
- **Next Steps**: Soften column shadow opacity slightly to maximize text legibility in the left ID photo area.

### 6. Louisiana (LA)
- **Composition & Layout**: Intricate French Quarter wrought-iron balcony filigree framing the top header, meandering Mississippi delta waterway curves flowing diagonally, and concentric ripple accents.
- **Findings**: The wrought-iron balcony scrollwork creates an immediately recognizable regional identity. Meandering waterways have good diagonal flow.
- **Next Steps**: Refine the interlocking S-scroll filigree resolution and balance gold gradient highlights.

### 7. Tennessee (TN)
- **Composition & Layout**: Four rolling Great Smoky Mountains misty ridge layers with vertical atmospheric haze graduation, undulating 5-line musical staff waves in gold/amber, and dogwood floral flourishes in the lower corners.
- **Findings**: Atmospheric perspective across the four mountain layers is effective. The 5-line musical staff wave bundle adds great thematic depth.
- **Next Steps**: Deepen the misty atmospheric gradient and soften dogwood petal line weights.

### 8. Utah (UT)
- **Composition & Layout**: Delicate Arch natural sandstone arch formation rising on the right flank with open negative-space window cutout, layered red rock canyon strata across the center, and distant Wasatch peaks.
- **Findings**: The Delicate Arch cutout is properly rendered with negative space, creating an authentic geological bridge. Sedimentary canyon strata provide rich horizontal texture.
- **Next Steps**: Refine arch strata line density and balance terracotta contrast.

### 9. Washington (WA)
- **Composition & Layout**: Mount Rainier volcanic cone dominating the center-right skyline, coastal evergreen conifers along the midground, and Puget Sound maritime wave ribbons in the foreground.
- **Findings**: Mount Rainier's volcanic shape is immediately recognizable. Conifer silhouettes along the foothill boundary enhance depth.
- **Next Steps**: Fine-tune glacier snowcap couloirs and maritime wave phase shifts.

### 10. Wyoming (WY)
- **Composition & Layout**: Jagged Grand Teton peaks across the northern skyline, High Plains wind streamline vectors sweeping through the sky, and western engraved corner flourishes.
- **Findings**: Teton peaks possess high visual impact. Wind streamlines effectively convey the open High Plains atmosphere.
- **Next Steps**: Calibrate wind streamline spacing and enhance peak shadow facet contrast.
