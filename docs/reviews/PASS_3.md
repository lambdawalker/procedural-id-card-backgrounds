## Executive Summary
Pass 3 specifically evaluated compliance with the strict geometry requirement:
> *"Snowcaps, mountain peaks, shading, and engraving must derive from the same underlying geometry. Never position independent triangles over a mountain by eye. Snow must follow and remain inside the actual mountain silhouette, including its irregular ridge edges. Apply the same principle to other layered motifs."*

Every mountain-bearing state (Alaska, Colorado, Idaho, Utah, Washington, Wyoming) was audited across high-resolution full-size renders and through the automated 5,122-point ray-tracing geometry test.
